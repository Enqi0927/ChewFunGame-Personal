# ChewFun Cooking Adventure

**ChewFun Cooking Adventure** is a 2D platformer game that I developed for my university project. The idea behind the game was to create an engaging platformer that also has an element of exploration. The player takes on the role of a chef who must navigate through various levels, collecting recipes while avoiding enemies and obstacles.

This project has been a fun and challenging experience, allowing me to improve my programming skills, particularly in game design and Java. It was also an opportunity to learn how to make the game mechanics more engaging while keeping things simple.

## Features:
- **Three Exciting Levels**: Each level is designed with a unique layout, presenting different challenges. The difficulty increases as the player progresses through the levels.
- **Player Mechanics**: The player can move left/right using arrow keys and jump with the **spacebar**. The player also interacts with items like Energy Drinks and Beef Noodles to collect them.
- **Enemies**: The game features several enemies like **angry peanuts**, **blenders**, and **microwaves** that the player must avoid or defeat. The collisions with these enemies cost the player a life.
- **Victory Screen**: Once the player collects the final recipe in Level 3, a victory screen appears, signalling the completion of the game.
- **Item Collection**: Collect items like **Energy Drinks** and **Beef Noodles** to help the player move forward in the game and increase their chances of surviving.

## Challenges:
- **Platform Layouts**: One of the main challenges I faced was designing the platform layouts. I had to balance the distance between platforms to ensure the jumps were not too easy or too difficult. Initially, I made the jumps too far apart, which made it frustrating for players. I later adjusted the platform positions to create a better balance, and that made a big difference in the gameplay experience.
- **Collision Detection**: Fine-tuning the collision detection between the player and the enemies was another challenge. At first, the player would sometimes pass through the enemies or take damage when they were not supposed to. After some debugging, I managed to get the collision system to work as expected.
- **Game Transitions**: I spent a lot of time refining the game transitions between levels, making sure that after collecting the recipe in each level, the game would seamlessly move to the next level without glitches or delays.

## Future Improvements:
- **More Levels**: I would love to add **more levels**, each with unique themes, enemies, and platform challenges. Each level would introduce new mechanics to keep the player engaged.
- **Enemy AI**: Currently, the enemies follow basic movement patterns, but I'd like to add **more complex AI** to make the enemies more challenging. This could include enemies that follow the player or avoid obstacles in interesting ways.
- **Sound and Music**: The game currently lacks sound effects and background music, which I plan to add in the future. This would greatly enhance the atmosphere and overall experience.
- **UI Improvements**: I plan to improve the **UI/UX** to make it cleaner and more visually appealing. A better menu system and HUD design would help the player better navigate through the game.
